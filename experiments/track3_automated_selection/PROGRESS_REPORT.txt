SUMMARY
-------
Track 3 focuses on developing systematic pipelines for selecting optimal judge 
sets. The goal is to programmatically identify redundant judges, propose 
complementary judges to fill gaps, and create reusable selection heuristics.

================================================================================

3.0 LLM-Driven Judge Decomposition Pipeline ✅
----------------------------------------------
Status: COMPLETED

A three-agent LLM pipeline that recursively decomposes parent judges into 
orthogonal sub-dimensions:

  - DecompositionAgent: Identifies 3-5 orthogonal sub-dimensions
  - BrainstormAgent: Authors detailed 5-level rubrics for each dimension  
  - ValidationAgent: Validates coverage and minimal overlap

Files:
  - judge_decomposition/llm_judge_decomposer.py
  - judge_decomposition/decompose_all_judges.py

Output: 55 hierarchical judges (10 parents + 45 children) with parent-child
relationships stored in YAML format.
